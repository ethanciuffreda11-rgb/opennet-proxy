Search or open any site through this proxy — works in a normal school browser tab. No install.

Tip: bookmark this page on your Chromebook so you can open it even if the home link is filtered later.

Made for school devices

  • Runs entirely in the browser — no apps or extensions to install (Chromebooks / managed laptops OK).
  • Uses HTTPS on Render so many school networks will load this host.
  • Pages are fetched by the proxy server, then shown to you, so filtering on the site’s real domain often doesn’t apply.

If your school blocks Render or this custom URL, ask for a different hostname or deploy under another free host. Some sites also break when heavily scripted — try a simpler page or search result first.
